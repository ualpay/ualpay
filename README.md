# Hi, I'm Umur Alpay 👋
I'm a Lead Developer based in Amsterdam, currently working at Shoparize. Most of my day-to-day work is building and maintaining e-commerce and advertising platform features, from backend services to the interfaces that sit on top of them.
## 👨‍💻 About Me
I work across the stack, but I spend a good chunk of my time on backend logic, APIs, and the plumbing that keeps a growing platform reliable. Shoparize operates in the price comparison and advertising space, so a lot of what I build touches feed management, integrations, and performance at scale. I like understanding a system end to end rather than just the part I happen to be assigned.
Outside of work I keep an eye on markets as a day trader, and I write occasionally about things I find interesting, technical and otherwise.
